Root Canal Treatment (RCT)

Save your natural tooth with single-visit or painless rotary root canal therapy. Removes infection deep inside the tooth root while eliminating severe ache.

  • Pain-free local anesthesia procedure
  • Prevents tooth extraction
  • High-precision ceramic crowns after RCT
⚡

Emergency Tooth Pain Relief

Immediate diagnostic assessment and emergency intervention for acute toothache, sensitivity, wisdom tooth swelling, or oral trauma in Bairamalguda.

  • Same-day emergency appointments
  • Targeted pain diagnosis
  • Gentle wisdom tooth evaluation

Teeth Cleaning & Scaling

Ultrasonic scaling to gently remove tough tartar buildup, plaque, and stains. Protects gums from bleeding (gingivitis) and ensures long-lasting fresh breath.

  • Ultrasonic plaque & calculus removal
  • Stain polishing for natural shine
  • Gum health assessment
👑

Dental Crowns & Fixed Bridges

Restore weak or broken teeth with natural-looking Zirconia or Ceramic crowns. Replace missing teeth with sturdy fixed bridges to restore your natural bite.

  • Natural shade matching
  • Durable, long-lasting materials
  • Custom bite alignment
🛡️

Preventive Care & Fluoride

Comprehensive dental checkups, cavity detection, tooth fillings (composite tooth-colored fillings), and enamel strengthening for long-term health.

  • Tooth-colored aesthetic fillings
  • Pediatric cavity prevention
  • Routine oral hygiene guidance
🔬

Dental Implants & Alignment

Permanent solution for missing teeth with bio-compatible dental implants. Consultations for invisible aligners, braces, and full smile makeovers.

  • Permanent root-and-crown replacement
  • Orthodontic alignment consultation
  • Natural chewing function restoration
